## Escalation Tiers
Customer issues are handled across three tiers: Tier 1 (branch/call center staff) for routine inquiries, Tier 2 (customer service desk) for unresolved or complex issues, and Tier 3 (management) for high-impact or reputational matters.

## When to Escalate to Tier 2
An issue is escalated to Tier 2 if it cannot be resolved within one business day, involves a financial discrepancy, or the customer explicitly requests escalation.

## When to Escalate to Tier 3
Tier 3 escalation applies to complaints involving potential regulatory breaches, media or reputational risk, or when a customer threatens legal action.

## Service Level Targets
Tier 1 issues should be resolved within 24 hours, Tier 2 within 3 business days, and Tier 3 within 5 business days, with the customer updated at each stage.
