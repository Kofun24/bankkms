## Logging a Complaint
Employees must log every complaint in the Complaint Management System within the same business day it is received, regardless of channel.

## Categorization
Complaints are categorized as service-related, product-related, fraud-related, or regulatory. Fraud-related complaints must be immediately flagged to the Fraud Investigation team.

## Resolution Timeline
Standard complaints must be resolved within 7 business days. Complaints involving suspected fraud or regulatory breaches are escalated and do not follow the standard timeline.

## Escalation Path
Unresolved complaints are escalated to the branch manager after 5 business days, and to the Customer Service Escalation desk if unresolved after 7 business days.
