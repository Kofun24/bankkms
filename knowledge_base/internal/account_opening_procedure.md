## Step 1: Initial Verification
The branch officer verifies the customer's identity document against the National Identity database before proceeding with any account opening request.

## Step 2: Risk Rating Assignment
Every new customer is assigned a risk rating (low, medium, high) using the internal risk scoring sheet, based on occupation, source of funds, and expected transaction volume.

## Step 3: System Entry
Account details are entered into the core banking system. A second officer must independently verify the entered data before the account is activated (maker-checker control).

## Step 4: Welcome Kit Issuance
Once activated, the branch issues a welcome kit containing the passbook or account statement schedule, debit card application, and mobile banking registration instructions.
