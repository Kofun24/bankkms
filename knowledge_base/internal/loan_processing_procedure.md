## Application Intake
Loan officers collect the completed application form, proof of income, bank statements for the last six months, and collateral documentation where applicable.

## Credit Assessment
The applicant's credit score, existing debt obligations, and repayment capacity are evaluated using the bank's standard credit scoring model before any approval decision is made.

## Approval Authority
Loan officers may approve loans up to LKR 1,000,000. Amounts above this threshold require branch manager approval, and amounts above LKR 5,000,000 require regional credit committee review.

## Disbursement
Once approved, funds are disbursed to the customer's account within two business days, and a formal loan agreement with repayment schedule is issued to the customer.
