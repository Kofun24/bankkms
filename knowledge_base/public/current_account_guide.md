## Overview
A current account is designed for customers and businesses with frequent transaction needs. It does not earn interest but offers unlimited withdrawals and deposits.

## Minimum Balance Requirement
The minimum average monthly balance for a personal current account is LKR 5,000. Business current accounts require a minimum average monthly balance of LKR 25,000.

## Overdraft Facility
Eligible customers may apply for a pre-approved overdraft facility, subject to a separate credit assessment. Interest is charged only on the utilized overdraft amount.

## Fees and Charges
A current account carries a monthly service fee of LKR 250, waived if the minimum average balance is maintained throughout the month.
