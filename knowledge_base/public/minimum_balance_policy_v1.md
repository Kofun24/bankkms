## Minimum Balance Requirement
The minimum balance required to maintain a personal savings account is LKR 500. Accounts falling below this threshold for more than 30 consecutive days are subject to a LKR 50 maintenance fee.

## Fee Waiver
The maintenance fee may be waived for students and senior citizens upon providing valid identification at any branch.
