## How do I check my account balance?
Customers can check their account balance through the mobile app, internet banking portal, any ATM, or by visiting a branch with a valid ID.

## How do I report a lost or stolen card?
Report a lost or stolen card immediately by calling the 24-hour card hotline or freezing the card instantly through the mobile app. A replacement card can be requested at any branch.

## Can I open an account online?
Yes, new savings and current accounts can be opened through the mobile app for individual customers who can complete KYC verification digitally. Business accounts must be opened in person at a branch.

## How long does a fund transfer take?
Transfers between accounts at the same bank are instant. Transfers to other banks via the national interbank network are typically completed within one business day.

## What should I do if I notice an unauthorized transaction?
Contact the bank immediately through the fraud hotline or mobile app to report the transaction. The account may be temporarily restricted while the matter is investigated.
