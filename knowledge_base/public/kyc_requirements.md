## Purpose
Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements exist to verify customer identity, assess risk, and prevent the bank from being used for financial crime.

## Required Documents
Individual customers must provide a valid National Identity Card or passport, and a recent utility bill or bank statement as proof of address dated within the last three months.

## Business Customer Requirements
Business customers must additionally provide a certificate of incorporation, a list of directors and beneficial owners, and a board resolution authorizing account opening.

## Periodic Review
KYC information is reviewed periodically based on the customer's risk rating: annually for high-risk customers, every three years for medium-risk, and every five years for low-risk customers.
