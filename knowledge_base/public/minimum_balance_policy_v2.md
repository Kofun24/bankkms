## Minimum Balance Requirement
The minimum balance required to maintain a personal savings account is LKR 1,000. Accounts falling below this threshold at any point during the month are subject to a LKR 100 maintenance fee, assessed monthly rather than after a 30-day grace period.

## Fee Waiver
The maintenance fee is waived for students, senior citizens, and salary account holders upon providing valid identification at any branch.
