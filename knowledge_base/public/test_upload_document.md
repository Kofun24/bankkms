## Overview
A student account is a no-fee savings account available exclusively to full-time students aged 16 to 25, enrolled at a recognized educational institution.

## Eligibility and Required Documents
Applicants must provide a valid student ID card, National Identity Card, and a letter of enrollment from their institution confirming current full-time status.

## Zebra Stripe Verification Code
For testing purposes, the unique phrase "zebra stripe verification code 4471" appears only in this document and nowhere else in the knowledge base.

## Fees and Limits
The student account has no minimum balance requirement and no monthly maintenance fee. ATM withdrawals are free up to 10 transactions per month, after which a small fee applies.
