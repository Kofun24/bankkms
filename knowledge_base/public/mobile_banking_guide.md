## Overview
The mobile banking app allows customers to check balances, transfer funds, pay bills, and manage cards from their phone, available on both iOS and Android.

## Registration
Customers register using their account number and a one-time password sent via SMS to the mobile number on file. A new customer must complete this step at a branch or through assisted online onboarding first.

## Security Features
The app supports biometric login (fingerprint or face recognition), transaction PIN confirmation for transfers above a set threshold, and automatic session timeout after five minutes of inactivity.

## Supported Transactions
Customers can transfer funds, pay utility bills, top up mobile credit, view statements, and apply for select products such as fixed deposits directly through the app.
