## Overview
The bank offers credit cards for everyday spending, with a revolving credit limit assigned based on the applicant's income and credit history.

## Eligibility and Application
Applicants must be at least 21 years old with a minimum monthly income of LKR 50,000. Required documents include proof of income, a National Identity Card, and three months of bank statements.

## Interest and Repayment
Interest is charged only on the outstanding balance after the due date. A minimum payment of 5% of the outstanding balance is required each month to avoid late payment fees.

## Lost or Stolen Cards
A lost or stolen credit card must be reported immediately through the 24-hour hotline or the mobile app, which can instantly freeze the card. The customer is not liable for unauthorized transactions reported within 48 hours of discovering the loss.
