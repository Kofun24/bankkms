## Right to Information
Customers have the right to receive clear, accurate information about fees, interest rates, and terms and conditions before opening any product.

## Right to Complain
Customers may lodge a complaint at any branch, via the call center, or through the mobile app. The bank must acknowledge a complaint within two business days.

## Right to Data Privacy
Customer personal and financial data will not be shared with third parties without consent, except where required by law or regulation.

## Right to Redress
If a complaint is not resolved to the customer's satisfaction, the customer may escalate it to the bank's internal ombudsman or the relevant regulatory authority.
