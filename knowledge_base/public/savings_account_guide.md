## Overview
A savings account is a deposit account that earns interest on the balance held. It is intended for customers who want to set aside funds while retaining easy access to them.

## Minimum Balance Requirement
Customers must maintain a minimum balance of LKR 1,000 at all times. If the balance falls below this threshold, a monthly maintenance fee of LKR 100 applies.

## Interest Rate
Interest is calculated daily on the closing balance and credited to the account quarterly. The standard interest rate is reviewed every six months and published on the bank's rate sheet.

## How to Open an Account
Customers can open a savings account at any branch or through the mobile app by providing a National Identity Card, proof of address, and an initial deposit of at least LKR 1,000.
