## Overview
A fixed deposit allows customers to invest a lump sum for a set period in exchange for a higher, guaranteed interest rate than a regular savings account.

## Tenure and Interest Rates
Fixed deposits are available for terms of 3, 6, 12, 24, and 36 months. Longer tenures generally earn higher interest rates, published on the bank's current rate sheet.

## Early Withdrawal
Withdrawing a fixed deposit before maturity is permitted but incurs a penalty, typically a reduction in the applicable interest rate for the period held.

## Maturity Options
At maturity, customers can choose to withdraw the full amount, renew the deposit for the same term, or renew with partial withdrawal.
