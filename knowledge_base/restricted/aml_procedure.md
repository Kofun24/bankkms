## Purpose and Scope
This procedure sets out the bank's anti-money laundering (AML) controls, applicable to all customer-facing and back-office staff involved in account opening, transaction monitoring, and reporting.

## Section 4.2: Suspicious Transaction Indicators
Staff must treat the following as red flags requiring escalation: transactions inconsistent with the customer's declared profile, structuring of deposits to avoid reporting thresholds, and rapid movement of funds through an account with no apparent business purpose.

## Reporting Obligations
Any transaction meeting a suspicious indicator must be reported to the Compliance Officer within 24 hours via a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR). Staff must not disclose to the customer that an STR has been filed.

## Recordkeeping
All AML-related records, including STRs and supporting evidence, must be retained for a minimum of seven years from the date of the transaction.
