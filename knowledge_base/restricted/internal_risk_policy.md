## Purpose
This policy establishes the bank's framework for identifying, assessing, and managing operational, credit, and reputational risk across all business units.

## Risk Appetite
The bank maintains a conservative risk appetite for credit exposure, capping single-borrower concentration at 15% of total lending capital and requiring quarterly portfolio stress testing.

## Risk Reporting
Business unit heads must submit a risk assessment report to the Chief Risk Officer monthly, with any material risk event reported within 48 hours regardless of the standard reporting cycle.

## Escalation to the Board
Risk events classified as Critical under the bank's severity matrix must be escalated to the Board Risk Committee within 72 hours of identification.
