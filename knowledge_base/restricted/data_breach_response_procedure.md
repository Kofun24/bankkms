## Purpose and Scope
This procedure governs the bank's response to any suspected or confirmed breach of customer or internal data, including unauthorized access, data leakage, or system compromise.

## Initial Detection and Containment
Upon detecting a suspected breach, the IT Security team must isolate affected systems within one hour and notify the Chief Information Security Officer immediately, before root cause analysis begins.

## Impact Assessment
The Compliance Officer and IT Security jointly assess the scope of affected data, the number of customers impacted, and whether the breach meets the threshold for regulatory notification under applicable data protection law.

## Regulatory and Customer Notification
Breaches meeting the regulatory threshold must be reported to the relevant data protection authority within 72 hours of confirmation. Affected customers must be notified without undue delay once the investigation confirms their data was compromised.

## Post-Incident Review
Within 30 days of resolution, a post-incident report documenting root cause, response timeline, and remediation steps must be filed with the Risk Committee.
