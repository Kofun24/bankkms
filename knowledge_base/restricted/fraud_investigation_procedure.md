## Initiating an Investigation
An investigation is opened as soon as a fraud report is received, whether from a customer, an employee, or an automated monitoring alert. The case is assigned a unique reference number.

## Evidence Collection
Investigators must preserve transaction logs, call recordings, CCTV footage where relevant, and any correspondence with the customer, before evidence retention windows expire.

## Interim Account Controls
Where fraud is suspected, the account may be temporarily restricted to prevent further loss, pending confirmation from the Fraud Investigation team. Customers must be notified of any restriction within 24 hours.

## Closure and Reporting
On closure, findings are documented in the case file and, where required, reported to the relevant regulator or law enforcement agency in line with statutory obligations.
